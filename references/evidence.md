# 证据索引与适用边界

核验截至2026-10-12。以下为决策相关来源，不是穷尽检索或正式GRADE评级。优先使用原始研究、系统综述、学会共识与官方应用指引；它们回答的问题不同，不能仅按文献类型决定一堂课是否适合个人。

引用时按文献ID定位并使用题名／DOI链接。若本次只核验摘要或条目，不宣称逐页阅读全文。百分比、频次、模板与进阶幅度若不是研究直接检验的协议，应称教练实施选择。实施新的补剂剂量、特殊人群处方或有争议建议时按需更新检索。

## 训练、测试与恢复

**T01｜长间歇的强度与工作量。** Seiler S, et al. *Adaptations to aerobic interval training: interactive effects of exercise intensity and total work duration.* Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports, 2013;23:74–83（在线2011）。[DOI: 10.1111/j.1600-0838.2011.01351.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0838.2011.01351.x)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21812820/)。35名受训休闲骑手、7周，比较4×4／8／16min。支持同时考虑强度和累计时间；并未验证人人4×8min、105–115%FTP或最后一组全力。摘要与协议信息核验，小样本、受训人群。

**T02｜训练强度分布。** Rosenblat MA, et al. *Which Training Intensity Distribution Intervention will Produce the Greatest Improvements in Maximal Oxygen Uptake and Time-Trial Performance in Endurance Athletes? A Systematic Review and Network Meta-analysis of Individual Participant Data.* Sports Medicine, 2025;55:655–673。[DOI: 10.1007/s40279-024-02149-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02149-3)；[作者机构条目与摘要](https://www.stir.ac.uk/research/hub/publication/2095779)。13项、348人，按心率区时间计算时，极化与金字塔总体未见明确差异。背景与分区统计影响解释；296男、52女，亚组结果不能变成个人必然收益。

**T03｜负荷与恢复。** Soligard T, et al. *How much is too much? (Part 1) International Olympic Committee consensus statement on load in sport and risk of injury.* British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2016;50:1030–1041。[DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2016-096581](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2016-096581)；[官方研究中心全文](https://ostrc.no/globalassets/publications/soligard_2016_bjsm_how-much-is-too-much.-ioc-consensus-statement-on-load-in-sport-and-risk-of-injury.pdf)。支持负荷、个人反应、生活压力共同监测。共识的观察性／团队运动证据不能证明统一加量率、恢复间隔或安全负荷公式。

**T04｜力量训练。** Llanos-Lagos C, Ramirez-Campillo R, Sáez de Villarreal E. *Heavy strength training effects on physiological determinants of endurance cyclist performance: a systematic review with meta-analysis.* European Journal of Applied Physiology, 2026;126:193–222（在线2025）。[DOI: 10.1007/s00421-025-05883-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-025-05883-2)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40632222/)。17项、262名受训骑手；部分效率、短时能力与表现结局获益，整体证据确定性偏低。不保证提高FTP，也不证明每天练核心或骑前固定激活动作必要。公开摘要核验。

**T05｜冲刺间歇。** Gist NH, et al. *Sprint interval training effects on aerobic capacity: a systematic review and meta-analysis.* Sports Medicine, 2014;44:269–279。[DOI: 10.1007/s40279-013-0115-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-013-0115-0)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24129784/)。支持冲刺间歇可作为特定刺激；有氧能力结局不等于FTP或专项成绩，不能证明截图复合课或每人都应冲刺。公开摘要核验。

**T06｜热身。** Fradkin AJ, Zazryn TR, Smoliga JM. *Effects of warming-up on physical performance: a systematic review with meta-analysis.* Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research, 2010;24:140–148。[DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0b013e3181c643a0](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e3181c643a0)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19996770/)。支持适当热身可能改善表现；项目与协议不同，不证明所有骑行统一10–20min。公开摘要核验。

**T07｜冷身与酸痛。** Law RYW, Herbert RD. *Warm-up reduces delayed onset muscle soreness but cool-down does not: a randomised controlled trial.* Australian Journal of Physiotherapy, 2007;53:91–95。[DOI: 10.1016/S0004-9514(07)70041-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-9514(07)70041-7)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17535144/)。52人、倒退下坡步行的离心任务，不能当骑行直接试验；限制“冷身保证防酸痛”的主张，也不证明冷身所有用途无效。公开摘要核验。

**T08｜睡眠。** Walsh NP, et al. *Sleep and the athlete: narrative review and 2021 expert consensus recommendations.* British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2021;55:356–368。[DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2020-102025](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2020-102025)；[期刊原文](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/55/7/356)。成人常见7–9h参考需个体化，训练者可能需更多；不是验证“7h＋训练时长×0.5”的公式。已核验相关共识建议。

**T09｜赛前减量。** Bosquet L, et al. *Effects of tapering on performance: a meta-analysis.* Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 2007;39:1358–1365。[DOI: 10.1249/MSS.0b013e31806010e0](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e31806010e0)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17762369/)。竞技人群赛前减量的汇总结论不能外推为所有人的日常恢复周周期，比赛减量与恢复周需区分。公开摘要核验。

**T10｜FTP估算误差。** Borszcz F, et al. *Functional Threshold Power in Cyclists: Validity of the Concept and Physiological Responses.* International Journal of Sports Medicine, 2018;39(10):737–742。[DOI: 10.1055/s-0044-101546](https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0044-101546)；[作者机构条目与接受稿](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/69657/)。23名受训男性；20min估算与60min功率个体一致性有限，估算FTP下维持时间差异大。不能把0.95系数当精确生理阈值或保证一小时能力。核验接受稿相关方法与结论。

**T11｜整堂RPE负荷。** Foster C, et al. *A new approach to monitoring exercise training.* Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research, 2001;15(1):109–115。[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11708692/)。以骑行与篮球任务比较整堂体感和心率负荷，支持用整堂RPE辅助个人日志。不等于功率TSS或直接测量组织损伤，也不支持跨人排名。本次核验公开摘要。

## 山地项目

**M01｜XCO需求。** Protzen G, Inoue A, Buzzachera C, et al. *The Physiology of Contemporary Olympic Cross-Country Mountain Biking: A Systematic Review.* Sports Medicine - Open, 2026。[DOI: 10.1186/s40798-026-00976-4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40798-026-00976-4)；[开放全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12936250/)。53项国家级至精英研究，支持有氧、反复高输出与技术共同作用。31项仅男性，偏描述性比赛需求；不能直接证明休闲骑手、XCM或重力项目的最佳课量。已核验研究范围与关键结果。

**M02｜DH需求。** Burr JF, Drury CT, Ivey AC, Warburton DER. *Physiological demands of downhill mountain biking.* Journal of Sports Sciences, 2012;30(16):1777–1785。[DOI: 10.1080/02640414.2012.718091](https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2012.718091)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23025296/)。11男、8女有经验骑手；握力下降及心率相对代谢需求偏高，支持零踩踏不等于恢复。小样本急性观察，不是Enduro处方或最佳训练剂量的干预试验。摘要及作者机构记录核验。

## 营养、补水与补剂

**N01｜运动营养总体框架。** Thomas DT, Erdman KA, Burke LM. *Position of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, Dietitians of Canada, and the American College of Sports Medicine: Nutrition and Athletic Performance.* Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, 2016;116:501–528。[DOI: 10.1016/j.jand.2015.12.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jand.2015.12.006)；[Dietitians of Canada官方全文](https://www.dietitians.ca/DietitiansOfCanada/media/Documents/Resources/noap-position-paper.pdf)。核验表1日碳水、恢复窗口与蛋白段落。指南范围按任务选择，非每个休闲骑手每日下限；快速糖原恢复的g/kg/h须保留小时单位。

**N02｜骑中碳水。** Australian Institute of Sport / Australian Sports Commission. *Sports drinks—How and when do I use it?* [官方指引](https://www.ausport.gov.au/ais/nutrition/supplements/group_a/sports-foods2/sports-drink/how-and-when-do-i-use-it)。核验按时长摄入表、多转运碳水与肠道训练。应用指引不能保证个人耐受，不应把长时高耗摄入量默认用于每次骑行。

**N03｜蛋白总量。** Jäger R, et al. *International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: protein and exercise.* Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition, 2017;14:20。[DOI: 10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8)；[期刊全文](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12970-017-0177-8)。核验1.4–2.0g/kg/day等建议。不少数据来自力量训练或短期肌蛋白合成，不能将每次摄入等同FTP收益；仍须考虑总能量与健康背景。

**N04｜营养时机。** Kerksick CM, et al. *International society of sports nutrition position stand: nutrient timing.* Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition, 2017;14:33。[DOI: 10.1186/s12970-017-0189-4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-017-0189-4)。核验每日足量优先、分餐与短恢复窗口语境。前餐、训练和下一次任务改变时机价值，不支持每次骑后都抢同一个狭窄窗口。

**N05｜补水个体化。** McDermott BP, et al. *National Athletic Trainers' Association Position Statement: Fluid Replacement for the Physically Active.* Journal of Athletic Training, 2017;52:877–895。[DOI: 10.4085/1062-6050-52.9.02](https://doi.org/10.4085/1062-6050-52.9.02)；[NATA官方全文](https://www.nata.org/sites/default/files/2025-08/fluid_replacement_for_the_physically_active.pdf)。核验表2汗率、过量饮水与水盐个体化。现场称量是近似值，单次结果不构成全年固定饮水率。

**N06｜能量可用性与REDs。** Mountjoy M, et al. *2023 International Olympic Committee’s (IOC) consensus statement on Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs).* British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2023;57:1073–1098。[DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2023-106994](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2023-106994)；[期刊全文](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/17/1073.long)。核验男女风险、供能与临床评估框架。大量证据为女性及观察研究；现场能量估算误差较大，信号用于复核和转介，不是远程诊断条件。

**N07｜补剂的用途与局限。** Maughan RJ, et al. *IOC consensus statement: dietary supplements and the high-performance athlete.* British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2018;52:439–455。[DOI: 10.1136/bjsports-2018-099027](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2018-099027)；[PMC条目](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5867441/)。核验摘要／全文索引：营养评估、特定用途和个体试用。精英竞技导向不是休闲骑手必买清单；本次全文访问遇验证码，未完整阅读。

**N08｜AIS A组。** AIS / ASC. *Sports Supplement Framework—Group A.* [官方分类](https://www.ausport.gov.au/ais/nutrition/supplements/group_a)。核验运动食品、表现补剂与医疗补充三类。A组表示特定用途证据，非每人必需；分类会更新。

**N09｜咖啡因。** Guest NS, et al. *International society of sports nutrition position stand: caffeine and exercise performance.* Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition, 2021;18:1。[DOI: 10.1186/s12970-020-00383-4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12970-020-00383-4)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33388079/)；[AIS低剂量说明](https://www.ausport.gov.au/ais/nutrition/supplements/group_a/performance-supplements2/caffeine/what-is-it)。核验成人低剂量2–3mg/kg可有效。平均效应不保证个人获益，睡眠与不良反应影响取舍，不外推儿童或孕产期。

**N10｜肌酸与耐力。** Fernández-Landa J, et al. *Effects of Creatine Monohydrate on Endurance Performance in a Trained Population: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.* Sports Medicine, 2023;53:1017–1027。[DOI: 10.1007/s40279-023-01823-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-023-01823-2)；[期刊摘要](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-023-01823-2)。13项研究总体未见明确耐力获益。不否定力量／短冲刺用途，也不能由机制承诺FTP增长。本次核验公开摘要。

**N11｜硝酸盐。** AIS / ASC. *Dietary Nitrate / Beetroot Juice—What does it look like?* [官方说明](https://www.ausport.gov.au/ais/nutrition/supplements/group_a/performance-supplements2/beetroot-juicenitrate/what-does-it-look-like)。核验标签应看硝酸盐含量、产品差异与研究剂量。硝酸盐mg不是甜菜根粉g，效果依人和任务，不应自行配制化学硝酸盐／亚硝酸盐。

**N12｜AIS B组。** AIS / ASC. *Sports Supplement Framework—Group B.* [官方分类](https://www.ausport.gov.au/ais/nutrition/supplements/group_b)。核验鱼油与姜黄素目前分类。新兴／混合证据不表示完全无效，也不支持人人常规服用或统一剂量；需按用途复核。

**N13｜谷氨酰胺。** Ramezani Ahmadi A, et al. *The effect of glutamine supplementation on athletic performance, body composition, and immune function: A systematic review and a meta-analysis of clinical trials.* Clinical Nutrition, 2019;38:1076–1091（在线2018）。[DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2018.05.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clnu.2018.05.001)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29784526/)。47项综述、25项荟萃分析，未支持稳定有氧／免疫增益。检索较早且异质，特殊医疗用途另论。本次核验摘要。

**N14｜CoQ10。** Deng H, et al. *Coenzyme Q10 supplementation increases blood concentrations but shows limited and inconsistent effects on exercise performance: a systematic review and meta-analysis.* British Journal of Nutrition, 2026;135(7):674–694（在线2025）。[DOI: 10.1017/S0007114525106211](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114525106211)；[Cambridge摘要](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-nutrition/article/abs/coenzyme-q10-supplementation-increases-blood-concentrations-but-shows-limited-and-inconsistent-effects-on-exercise-performance-a-systematic-review-and-metaanalysis/E0BC4A76C08C59B7706D739FEAB60EED)。24项随机试验、混合成人任务，效果有限且不一致、证据确定性低。血浓度变化不是FTP或比赛收益。本次主要核验摘要与书目信息。

## 性别与生理阶段

**G01｜月经周期与表现。** McNulty KL, Elliott-Sale KJ, Dolan E, et al. *The Effects of Menstrual Cycle Phase on Exercise Performance in Eumenorrheic Women: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.* Sports Medicine, 2020;50:1813–1827。[DOI: 10.1007/s40279-020-01319-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-020-01319-3)；[期刊原文](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-020-01319-3)。核验78项、平均效应很小、异质且质量有限。自然规律月经、18–40岁、无激素避孕样本，不验证统一周期课表，也不否定个体症状。

**G02｜女性运动营养。** Sims ST, Kerksick CM, Smith-Ryan AE, et al. *International society of sports nutrition position stand: nutritional concerns of the female athlete.* Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition, 2023;20(1):2204066。[DOI: 10.1080/15502783.2023.2204066](https://doi.org/10.1080/15502783.2023.2204066)；[PubMed摘要](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37221858/)；[PMC入口](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10210857/)。核验摘要与索引：能量、生命阶段、碳水耐受与铁风险。部分阶段建议依据有限数据与机制，不应写成所有女性统一菜单；未逐页阅读全文。

## 测量与无功率执行

**D01｜模拟功率。** 华为支持：[华为手表骑行模拟功率不准确](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16066750/)。已读官方算法说明：速度、海拔、个人与车辆信息参与估算，跟骑等影响准确性。用于区分估算和传感器测量，不作为全品牌精度比较或训练干预证据；其他设备需查相应说明。

**D02｜腕式心率。** 华为支持：[华为穿戴设备心率检测不准确](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn00733859/)。已读佩戴、手腕活动、低温与光学测量影响。官方说明不证明所有手表都同样准确，也不能以测量问题解释所有运动不适。

**D03｜RPE与说话线索。** British Cycling. [Rate of Perceived Exertion Scale—RPE](https://www.britishcycling.org.uk/knowledge/training-plans/supporting-documents/article/izn20151119-Training-Plans-Supporting-Documents-Related-Rate-of-Perceived-Exertion-Scale---RPE-0/undefined)；ACSM. [Tips for Monitoring Aerobic Exercise Intensity](https://www.acsm.org/docs/default-source/files-for-resource-library/exercise-intensity-infographic.pdf)。核验官方公开索引与图表：可用体感与交谈辅助执行。不同量表起点／描述不同，技能使用明确的0–10沟通标尺；不把分数精确换算FTP区、乳酸阈值或个人心率。British Cycling部分直接访问返回403。
